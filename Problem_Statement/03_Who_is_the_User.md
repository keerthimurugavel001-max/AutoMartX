# 03. Who is the User

# 🚗 AutoMartX – AI-Powered Smart Vehicle Marketplace

---

## Overview

AutoMartX is designed for **three primary user groups**, each with distinct goals, responsibilities, and permissions within the platform. The system is built around their specific needs to ensure a smooth, trustworthy, and intelligent marketplace experience.

## 👤 1. Buyer

The Buyer is anyone looking to purchase a vehicle through the platform. Instead of relying solely on seller descriptions, buyers use AI-powered insights to make confident, informed decisions.

### Who they are
- Individuals searching for a new or used vehicle
- First-time buyers who need guidance on fair pricing
- Experienced buyers comparing multiple options before purchase

### What they can do
- Register and log in securely
- Browse and search vehicles with advanced filters
- Compare multiple vehicles side by side
- Save favourite vehicles to a wishlist
- Contact sellers directly
- Book vehicle inspections
- View AI-generated price predictions
- View AI Vehicle Health Score
- Check Seller Trust Score before engaging
- Receive personalized vehicle recommendations
- Submit reviews after a transaction

### Their core need
Confidence that the vehicle they buy is fairly priced, in good condition, and sold by a trustworthy seller.

---

## 🚘 2. Seller

The Seller is anyone listing a vehicle for sale on the platform — from individual owners to small dealerships.

### Who they are
- Individual vehicle owners selling their personal vehicle
- Small-scale used-car dealers
- Verified dealerships listing multiple vehicles

### What they can do
- Create and manage vehicle listings
- Upload multiple vehicle images
- Update vehicle information as needed
- Manage buyer bookings and inspection requests
- Respond to buyer enquiries
- View listing performance analytics
- Receive AI suggestions to improve listing quality
- Verify ownership documents to build trust

### Their core need
A platform that helps their listings stand out, build buyer trust quickly, and reduces the effort of managing enquiries and bookings.

---

## 🛡️ 3. Administrator

The Administrator is responsible for maintaining the integrity, security, and smooth operation of the entire marketplace.

### Who they are
- Platform operators / internal staff
- Moderators responsible for content and user oversight

### What they can do
- Manage all user accounts (buyers and sellers)
- Verify and approve sellers
- Approve or reject vehicle listings
- Remove fraudulent or duplicate content
- View platform-wide analytics dashboards
- Manage vehicle categories
- Handle reported listings and user complaints
- Monitor overall platform activity
- Generate business and usage reports

### Their core need
Full visibility and control over the platform to ensure it remains safe, trustworthy, and free of fraudulent activity.

---

## Why Understanding These Users Matters

Designing AutoMartX around these three distinct user roles ensures that:

- Buyers get transparency and confidence before purchasing.
- Sellers get tools that make listing and selling easier and more credible.
- Administrators can maintain a safe, fraud-free marketplace at scale.

> Every feature in AutoMartX — from AI price prediction to trust scoring — exists to directly serve the needs of these three user groups.
