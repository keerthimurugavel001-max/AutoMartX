# 02. Domain

# 🚗 AutoMartX – AI-Powered Smart Vehicle Marketplace

---

## Primary Domain

**Automotive E-Commerce & Artificial Intelligence**

AutoMartX operates at the intersection of the automotive resale industry and modern AI-driven web technology, combining online marketplace functionality with intelligent decision-support systems.

---

## Domain Categories

### 🚘 Automotive / Vehicle Resale
The core business domain — buying and selling used and new vehicles (cars, bikes, and other automobiles) through a digital platform.

### 🛒 E-Commerce & Online Marketplace
AutoMartX functions as a two-sided marketplace connecting buyers and sellers, similar in structure to e-commerce platforms, but specialized for high-value vehicle transactions.

### 🤖 Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
AI is used for price prediction, vehicle health scoring, fraud/fake listing detection, and personalized recommendations — placing the project firmly within applied AI/ML.

### 📊 Data Analytics
Vehicle data (brand, model, year, mileage, condition) is analyzed to generate insights such as market trends, pricing accuracy, and seller behavior patterns.

### 🔐 Trust & Safety / FinTech-adjacent
Seller verification, trust scoring, and fraud detection align the project with trust-and-safety systems commonly seen in FinTech and marketplace security domains.

### 🌐 Full-Stack Web Development
The platform is built as a complete web application with a frontend, backend, database, and cloud deployment — covering the full software development lifecycle.

---

## Sub-Domains Involved

| Sub-Domain | Application in AutoMartX |
|---|---|
| Web Development | React.js frontend + FastAPI backend |
| Database Management | PostgreSQL relational schema design |
| Machine Learning | Price prediction, health scoring, fraud detection models |
| Cloud Computing | AWS deployment, Cloudinary storage |
| Geolocation Services | Google Maps API for nearby vehicle discovery |
| Authentication & Security | JWT-based secure login system |
| UI/UX Design | Tailwind CSS-based responsive interface |

---

## Industry Relevance

The global used-vehicle marketplace is a large and growing industry facing persistent challenges around pricing transparency, fraudulent listings, and buyer trust. AutoMartX is designed to address these real-world industry pain points using modern AI and web technologies, making it directly relevant to:

- Automotive resale platforms (e.g., OLX Autos, CarDekho, Cars24 style platforms)
- Online classifieds and marketplace systems
- AI-powered valuation and recommendation engines

---

## Why This Domain Was Chosen

- High real-world relevance and demand for trustworthy vehicle marketplaces.
- Strong opportunity to apply Artificial Intelligence in a practical, measurable way (pricing, health scoring, fraud detection).
- Combines multiple technical disciplines — web development, databases, AI/ML, and cloud deployment — making it a comprehensive academic and portfolio project.
- Addresses a genuine problem (fraud, mispricing, lack of transparency) rather than being a purely theoretical exercise.

---

## Domain Summary

> AutoMartX belongs to the **Automotive E-Commerce domain**, enhanced with **Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics**, and delivered through a **full-stack web application** — making it a technically diverse, industry-relevant capstone project.
