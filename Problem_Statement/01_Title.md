---

## Project Description

AutoMartX is a next-generation AI-powered vehicle marketplace designed to make buying and selling vehicles safer, smarter, and more transparent.

Unlike traditional vehicle marketplace platforms that only display vehicle listings, AutoMartX helps users make informed decisions using Artificial Intelligence. The platform analyzes vehicle information, estimates a fair market price, evaluates the vehicle's condition, detects suspicious or fake listings, and measures seller credibility before a buyer makes a purchase.

The system combines a modern online marketplace with intelligent decision-support features to improve trust between buyers and sellers.

---

## Why the Name "AutoMartX"?

The project name **AutoMartX** represents three core ideas:

- **Auto** – Represents automobiles and the automotive industry.
- **Mart** – Represents a digital marketplace where users can buy and sell vehicles.
- **X** – Represents the next generation of innovation, Artificial Intelligence, smart automation, and advanced technology.

Together, the name symbolizes a modern, intelligent vehicle marketplace that goes beyond traditional buying and selling platforms.
